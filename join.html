Membership

Join NTU Semiconductor Club

Master full-stack semiconductors—from circuit design and fab operations to embedded programming, GPU computing, and AI hardware.

Member Benefits

Chip Design & VLSI

Workshops on digital/analog IC design, EDA tools, and processor architectures.

Foundry & Fab Access

Priority registration for industrial site visits to semiconductor cleanrooms and testing centers.

Embedded & RTOS

Coding sessions covering microcontroller firmware, embedded C/C++, and hardware interfaces.

GPU & AI Acceleration

Hands-on projects in CUDA GPU parallel programming and AI model deployment on hardware.

Who Can Join?

We welcome NTU undergraduate and postgraduate students from all disciplines.

Electrical & Electronic Engineering

For students interested in IC design, VLSI, microelectronics, and device fabrication.

Computer Engineering & Science

For students interested in computer architecture, embedded firmware, GPU programming (CUDA), and AI systems.

Materials Engineering & Physics

For students interested in semiconductor materials, thin films, quantum devices, and fab chemistry.

All Other Majors

Anyone passionate about semiconductor technology, hardware engineering, or tech leadership is welcome!
