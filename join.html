Membership

Join Us

Open to all NTU undergraduate and postgraduate students interested in microelectronics, hardware, and engineering.

Member Benefits

Factory Visits

Priority registration for site visits to semiconductor foundries and research labs.

Technical Workshops

Access to student workshops covering IC design, device physics, and cleanroom fabrication.

Industry Network

Opportunities to meet engineers, recruiters, and alumni working in semiconductor companies.

Student Community

Connect with peers across EEE, Computer Engineering, Materials Engineering, and Physics.

Who Can Join?

We welcome NTU students from all schools and majors.

Electrical & Electronic Engineering

Students interested in IC design, microelectronics, VLSI, and device fabrication.

Computer Engineering & Science

Students interested in computer architecture, hardware design, and embedded systems.

Materials Engineering & Physics

Students interested in semiconductor materials, thin films, and device physics.

All Other Disciplines

Any NTU student curious about the semiconductor industry is welcome to join.

How To Join

1

Email or Register

Send an email to ntu.semiconductor.club@ntu.edu.sg or fill out our sign-up form during recruitment drives.

2

Get Updates

Receive event announcements, registration links, and site visit invites.

3

Attend Activities

Participate in plant tours, technical workshops, and club social events.
