Open Admissions

WHO CAN JOIN?

We welcome undergraduate and postgraduate students from all NTU schools and disciplines interested in microelectronics and technology.

Electrical & Electronic Engineering

Explore semiconductor physics, integrated circuit (IC) design, VLSI, signal processing, and fab processes.

Computer Engineering & Science

Discover computer architecture, hardware security, AI accelerators, embedded systems, and silicon systems.

Materials Engineering & Physics

Study advanced semiconductor materials, nanoscale synthesis, quantum devices, and thin-film deposition.

All Other Disciplines

Anyone enthusiastic about semiconductor technology, business management, or technology leadership is welcome!
