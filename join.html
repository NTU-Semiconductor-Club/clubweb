Why Join the Club

Practical engineering exposure spanning wafer fabrication, design automation, and processor architectures.

Foundry & Cleanroom Access

Priority allocation for plant visits at AMD, GlobalFoundries, A*STAR IME, and NTU N2FC cleanrooms.

Technical Workshops

Hands-on sessions on EDA layout tools, microcontroller firmware, CUDA compute, and digital IC simulation.

Industry Networking

Direct coffee chats, resume reviews, and panel sessions with practicing engineers from the semiconductor industry.

Multidisciplinary Peer Community

Work alongside peers focused on silicon hardware, compilers, computer systems, and microelectronics.
