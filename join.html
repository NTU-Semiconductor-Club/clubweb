Why Join NTU Semiconductor Club?

Gain practical exposure across the entire semiconductor stack.

Exclusive Industry Visits

Priority access to company site visits at AMD, GlobalFoundries, A*STAR, and more.

Hands-on Workshops

Technical sessions on EDA tools, embedded programming, CUDA, and circuit design.

Career Networking

Coffee chats and career panels with engineers from the semiconductor industry.

Peer Community

Connect with fellow students passionate about hardware, chips, and computing.

Who Can Join?

We welcome undergraduate and postgraduate students from all NTU schools and majors.

Open to all NTU students regardless of faculty or year. Whether you study EEE, SCSE, MSE, Physics, or any other discipline — everyone is welcome.
