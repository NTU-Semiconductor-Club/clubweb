Why Join NTU Semiconductor Club?

Gain practical exposure across the entire semiconductor stack.

Chip Design & VLSI

Workshops on digital/analog IC design, EDA tools, and computer architecture layout.

Cleanroom & Fab Visits

Priority registration for industrial site visits to semiconductor foundries and research labs.

Embedded & Firmware

Coding sessions covering microcontroller firmware, embedded C/C++, and hardware interfaces.

GPU & AI Acceleration

Hands-on projects in CUDA GPU parallel programming and AI model deployment on silicon.

Who Can Join?

We welcome undergraduate and postgraduate students from all NTU schools and majors.

Electrical & Electronic Engineering

Students interested in IC design, VLSI, microelectronics, and device fabrication.

Computer Engineering & Science

Students interested in computer architecture, embedded firmware, GPU programming (CUDA), and AI systems.

Materials Engineering & Physics

Students interested in semiconductor materials, thin films, quantum devices, and fab chemistry.

All Other Majors

Anyone curious about hardware engineering, semiconductor technology, or leadership is welcome!
