About Us

Who We Are

A student-led organization at Nanyang Technological University created to help students explore semiconductor technology and career paths.

Bridging Classroom Learning & Industry Practice

We work with semiconductor foundries, design houses, and research institutes to organize plant tours, workshops, and student networking events.

Our Purpose

Semiconductor engineering spans multiple fields, from chip design and materials science to cleanroom fabrication. We give NTU students direct access to industry engineers and real manufacturing facilities so they can see how microelectronics are designed and produced.

Vision

To build an active community at NTU where engineering and science students can learn about semiconductors and prepare for careers in hardware and microelectronics.

Mission

To organize practical workshops, industrial site visits, and career talks that connect students directly with semiconductor companies and researchers.

Main Activities

Factory & Lab Visits

Guided tours of semiconductor fabs, cleanrooms, and testing facilities in Singapore.

Hands-on Workshops

Short courses covering IC design basics, fabrication principles, and testing equipment.

Peer Network

Connecting students from EEE, Computer Engineering, Materials Engineering, and Physics.

Career Talks

Sessions with engineers and recruiters sharing internship and graduate job opportunities.
