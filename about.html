Who We Are

ABOUT OUR CLUB

Empowering students to lead and innovate across the global microelectronics landscape.

BUILDING THE NEXT GENERATION OF
SEMICONDUCTOR INNOVATORS

NTU Semiconductor Club is a premier student-led organization connecting students, academia, and industry leaders to shape the future of chip technology.

Bridging Theory & Industry

We bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world industrial practice. By providing students with direct access to leading foundries, research institutes, and engineering leaders, we equip members with actionable insights and technical depth.

OUR VISION

Cultivating an interdisciplinary "Full Stack" semiconductor community that prepares NTU students for innovation and leadership across the entire silicon value chain—from materials and IC design to fabrication and software integration.

OUR MISSION

To accelerate technical development, facilitate industry access, and cultivate career pathways for students through high-impact workshops, company visits, technical projects, and professional networking sessions.

Core Pillars

WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Industry Engagement

Connecting students directly with top global semiconductor companies, foundry operators, and research labs through visits and leadership sessions.

Technical Mastery

Developing practical chip design, packaging, and microelectronics knowledge through hands-on workshops and expert masterclasses.

Vibrant Community

Creating a collaborative platform for students across EEE, Computer Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, and related fields.

Career Acceleration

Helping students navigate semiconductor career pathways, internship placements, and industry mentorship opportunities.
