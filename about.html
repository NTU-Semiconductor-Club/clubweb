About the Club

A student-run organization at NTU bridging hardware design, fabrication technology, and software development.

What We Do

We bring together students interested in microelectronics to learn through industry visits, technical workshops, and peer projects.

Vision

To give NTU students early exposure to hardware design, foundry operations, and high-performance computing.

Mission

To host cleanroom tours, industry site visits, hands-on workshops, and networking sessions with semiconductor companies.

Core Learning Pillars

Industry Site Visits

Tours of AMD, GlobalFoundries, A*STAR IME, and N2FC facilities to see real chip manufacturing and testing.

Cleanroom Access

Hands-on cleanroom sessions at NTU N2FC covering photolithography, wafer processing, and metrology.

Technical Workshops

Practical sessions on EDA tools, embedded firmware, CUDA programming, and circuit design.

Networking & Careers

Coffee chats, career panels, and connections with engineers from the semiconductor industry.
