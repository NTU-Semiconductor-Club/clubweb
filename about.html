The Full-Stack Semiconductor Vision

A student-led organization at Nanyang Technological University dedicated to teaching full-stack semiconductors—from silicon design and fab to embedded, GPU, and AI programming.

Bridging Hardware Design, Fabrication & Software Use

Semiconductor technology requires engineers who understand the full lifecycle: designing circuits, fabricating silicon, and programming the embedded systems, GPUs, and AI models that run on them.

Vision

To build an interdisciplinary club preparing NTU students for careers across the entire semiconductor value chain—design, fabrication, packaging, and high-level software applications.

Mission

To organize practical workshops, industrial site visits, cleanroom tours, and coding sessions covering hardware design, fab processes, embedded microcontrollers, and GPU/AI programming.

Core Learning Pillars

IC Design & VLSI

Digital logic, analog circuits, EDA tools, and computer architecture layout.

Fabrication & Cleanroom

Wafer fab tours, lithography, materials science, and semiconductor packaging.

Embedded & RTOS

Embedded C/C++, microcontrollers, sensor interfaces, and hardware programming.

GPU & AI Programming

GPU parallel programming (CUDA), AI model acceleration, and software-hardware optimization.
