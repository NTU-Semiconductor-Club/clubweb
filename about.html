About Us

The Full-Stack Vision

A student-led organization at Nanyang Technological University dedicated to teaching full-stack semiconductors—from silicon design and fab to embedded, GPU, and AI programming.

Bridging Hardware Design, Fabrication & Software Use

Semiconductor technology goes beyond single chips. We prepare students for the full lifecycle: designing circuits, fabricating silicon, and programming the embedded systems, GPUs, and AI models that run on them.

Why "Full-Stack" Semiconductor?

Modern microelectronics requires engineers who understand both hardware and software. By exposing students to IC design, foundry fabrication, embedded microcontrollers, GPU computing (CUDA/HIP), and AI acceleration, we build well-rounded engineering leaders ready for industry.

Vision

Cultivating an interdisciplinary "Full Stack" club to prepare NTU students for innovation and careers across the entire semiconductor value chain—design, fabrication, packaging, and high-level software application.

Mission

To organize practical workshops, industrial site visits, cleanroom tours, and coding sessions covering hardware design, fab processes, embedded systems, and GPU/AI programming.

Core Learning Pillars

IC Design & VLSI

Digital logic, analog circuits, EDA tools, and computer architecture layout.

Fabrication & Cleanroom

Wafer fab tours, lithography, materials science, and semiconductor packaging.

Embedded & RTOS

Embedded C/C++, microcontrollers, sensor interfaces, and hardware programming.

GPU & AI Programming

GPU parallel programming (CUDA), AI model acceleration, and hardware-software optimization.
