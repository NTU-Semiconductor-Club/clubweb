The Full-Stack Semiconductor Curriculum

What our members learn and do.

Chip Design & VLSI

Learn digital logic, analog circuits, and the EDA tools used in real chip design.

Fabrication & Packaging

Visit cleanrooms and understand wafer fabrication, photolithography, and packaging.

Embedded Systems & Firmware

Write C/C++ firmware for microcontrollers and hardware interfaces.

GPU & AI Programming

Write parallel CUDA code to accelerate neural networks and heavy workloads.
