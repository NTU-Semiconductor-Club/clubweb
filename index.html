The "Full-Stack" Semiconductor Paradigm

Covering the complete journey from silicon chemistry to system-level software.

1. Chip Design & Architecture

IC design fundamentals, VLSI logic, digital/analog layouts, and computer architecture for CPUs, GPUs, and custom accelerators.

2. Fabrication & Packaging

Wafer fabrication steps, lithography, thin-film deposition, cleanroom protocols, and advanced semiconductor packaging.

3. Embedded Systems & Firmware

Embedded C/C++, microcontrollers, real-time operating systems (RTOS), and hardware-software co-design.

4. GPU & AI Acceleration

GPU parallel programming (CUDA/HIP), neural network acceleration on silicon, and high-performance computing applications.
