The Full-Stack Semiconductor Curriculum

Learning opportunities covering hardware engineering through software application.

Chip Design & VLSI

Digital logic, analog layout, computer architecture, and EDA tool fundamentals for CPUs, GPUs, and custom silicon.

Fabrication & Packaging

Wafer fab processes, lithography equipment, cleanroom protocols, and semiconductor packaging techniques.

Embedded Systems & Firmware

Microcontroller programming, embedded C/C++, real-time operating systems (RTOS), and hardware-software integration.

GPU & AI Programming

GPU parallel programming (CUDA/HIP), neural network hardware acceleration, and high-performance compute applications.
