Industrial Site Visits & Cleanroom Tours

Highlights from our AY2025/26 Semester 2 industry visits and fab tours.

27 MARCH 2026

A*STAR IME Industry Visit

Visited A*STAR IME to learn about advanced packaging and silicon photonics. The tour covered their photonics lab and packaging research facilities.

Silicon Photonics Advanced Packaging MEMS
25 FEBRUARY 2026

AMD Industry Visit

Students visited AMD Singapore to observe real-world chip testing and bring-up. The tour covered System Level Test (SLT), Final Test, Failure Analysis, and AMD's CPU, GPU, and datacenter accelerator portfolio.

System Level Test (SLT) Final Test Failure Analysis
26 JAN & 23 FEB 2026

N2FC Fab Tour

Hosted two back-to-back cleanroom visits to the Nanyang NanoFabrication Centre (N2FC). Members toured the N2FC cleanroom and saw photolithography machines and wafer processing tools in action.

Cleanroom Class 100/1000 Photolithography Wafer Tools
AY2025/26

GlobalFoundries Visit

Students toured commercial wafer fab facilities, observing silicon manufacturing operations, foundry engineering roles, and yield analysis in high-volume production.

Commercial Fab Line Automated Cleanroom Yield Analysis
2 APRIL 2026

Networking Coffee Chat

An informal coffee chat where members discussed internships, career paths in semiconductors, and upcoming club plans.

Engineer Mentorship Career Guidance
22 JANUARY 2026

AI Career Launch Pad (with AI Singapore)

Partnered with AI Singapore (AISG) to demonstrate how hardware and systems knowledge translates into AI careers. Students were introduced to the AIIP and AIAP apprenticeship programs.

AI Singapore (AISG) AIIP & AIAP Systems & AI
