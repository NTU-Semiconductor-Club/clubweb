Highlights from our AY2025/26 Semester 2 foundry visits and research lab tours.

10 FEB 2026 • R&D TOUR

A*STAR IME Industry Visit

Toured the Institute of Microelectronics (IME) at Science Park II. Learned about advanced silicon photonics, high-density 2.5D/3D packaging, and semiconductor metrology.

SILICON PHOTONICS ADVANCED PACKAGING
6 FEB 2026 • LAB EXPEDITION

AMD Singapore Industry Visit

Technical visit to AMD Singapore. Students inspected the System Level Test (SLT) floor, failure analysis labs, and learned about GPU architecture and post-silicon validation.

SYSTEM LEVEL TEST CHIP BRING-UP
26 JAN & 23 FEB 2026 • CLEANROOM

N2FC Fab Tour

Two cleanroom visits to Nanyang NanoFabrication Centre (N2FC). Members donned cleanroom suits and observed ultraviolet photolithography equipment and wafer processing tools in action.

CLASS 100/1000 CLEANROOM PHOTOLITHOGRAPHY

GlobalFoundries Visit

Toured commercial wafer fabrication lines, observing automated cassette handling, semiconductor yield engineering, and high-volume wafer production.

COMMERCIAL FAB YIELD ANALYSIS

Networking Coffee Chat

Informal technical chat where members connected with senior engineers to discuss fab engineering internships, IC design careers, and club project roadmaps.

ENGINEER MENTORSHIP CAREER GUIDANCE
